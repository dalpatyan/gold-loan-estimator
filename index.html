Concept prototype

Gold loan estimator

An accurate, explained loan estimate before a doorstep visit, and eligibility checks before a visit is booked, so fewer visits end without a loan.

By Gaurav Kumar · simulated data and checks · not affiliated with any lender

Try it

  1. Add your gold and see the estimate. Tap Loan details for the full working.
  2. Tap Book a free doorstep visit. Any 10-digit mobile number starting with 6 to 9 works, with any 6-digit code.
  3. Enter a personal PAN such as ABCPK1234F, allow the checks, and tap Check details.
Each result gets a different message, because the rules allow different things to be said.

The idea

Problem. Doorstep gold-loan apps often show a best-case number before the visit, so the gap between what customers expect and what their gold supports only shows up at the door. A visit that ends without a loan still costs the full visit.

Hypothesis. If customers see an accurate, explained estimate and are checked before a slot is booked, the right customers book, and more doorstep visits end in a loan.

Test. Show the new journey to half of app users for four weeks. Main metric: share of visits that end in a loan. Also watch the booking rate, and how often the final amount lands within the estimate.

Rules built in

  • Loan at 75% of gold value, below RBI's maximum, leaving a buffer if the gold price falls.
  • Coins count up to 50 g and jewellery up to 1 kg; bars can't be pledged.
  • Wilful defaulters are declined with the reason and how to dispute it.
  • A possible AML or sanctions match gets a neutral call-back, with no reason given, to avoid tipping off.
  • If a PAN can't be found, initial checks run on name, date of birth and mobile number. A red flag there leads to a call, never a decline, because a name match can be the wrong person.

Prototyped with Claude. Gold price, rates, codes, map and checks are simulated for illustration.
